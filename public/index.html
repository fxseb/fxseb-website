VI · QUESTIONS

Answered.

How are the results verified?+

Every strategy runs on a live account that is tracked on Myfxbook, the industry standard for independent verification. Myfxbook confirms both the track record and the trading privileges, so the results are pulled directly from the broker and cannot be edited. The data updates automatically.

Where is my capital held?+

Your capital stays in your own account at the broker. I never receive or hold client funds.

Do you sell your EAs or signals?+

No. My strategies are not for sale. They are only available through my partner's PAMM accounts.

What are the risks?+

Trading leveraged products such as CFDs is risky, and losses are part of every strategy. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Only allocate capital you can afford to lose.

Can I speak with you directly?+

Yes. Members of the private circle can join regular live calls, and larger allocations are supported personally through my partner's team.
