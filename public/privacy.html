Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit this website, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Sebastiano Cusumano

Email: contact@fxseb.com

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., 101 2nd Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA. When you visit the website, Netlify automatically processes technical data that your browser transmits, such as your IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, browser type and operating system (server log files).

This processing is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Data may be processed in the United States. Netlify relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure an adequate level of protection. Server logs are stored only for a limited period by the host.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, advertising trackers or social media plugins. No user profiles are created.

4. Fonts and media

All fonts, images and audio files are hosted on the same server as this website. No connection to third-party font or media providers (such as Google Fonts) is established when you visit this website.

5. External links

This website contains links to external services, for example Instagram or Myfxbook. Data is only transmitted to these services if you actively click a link. From that point, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

6. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the data you provide (such as your email address, name and message) will be processed solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. Your data will be deleted once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data concerning you (Art. 15)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • erasure (Art. 17)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • data portability (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the website or the legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
